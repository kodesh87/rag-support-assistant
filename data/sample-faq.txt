What is Nimbus Cloud?

Nimbus Cloud is a virtual private server (VPS) hosting provider. We offer pre-configured cloud servers so you can deploy websites, bots, game servers, and other applications without managing physical hardware.

What makes Nimbus Cloud good?

Nimbus Cloud runs on NVMe SSD storage across multiple regions, with a control panel that lets you reinstall, snapshot, or resize a server in a couple of clicks. Support is available over live chat and email, and most tickets get a first response within an hour.

How fast is Nimbus Cloud?

Our standard plans run on dedicated CPU cores (not oversubscribed vCPUs), and every server ships with NVMe storage by default, which gives noticeably faster disk I/O than spinning-disk or shared-vCPU competitors.

Which server locations are available?

We currently offer servers in Singapore, Jakarta, Tokyo, and Frankfurt. You can pick the region during checkout, and you can also deploy additional servers in a different region from your existing account.

Can I copy-paste into the console, and how?

Yes. Open your server from the dashboard, click "Console," and use Ctrl+Shift+V (or right-click and choose Paste) to paste text into the in-browser terminal. If you're connecting over SSH instead, copy-paste works the same as any normal terminal on your own machine.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes, all new accounts come with a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days, contact help@nimbuscloud.dev and we'll process a full refund.

How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

From the dashboard, go to your server's "Plan" tab and choose a new plan. Upgrades apply immediately after a resize and reboot; downgrades may require freeing up disk space first if the new plan has a smaller disk.

What operating systems do you support?

We support most common Linux distributions (Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux, Rocky Linux) as one-click installs, plus a custom ISO upload option for anything else.

I forgot my server's root password. What do I do?

From the dashboard, open your server and choose "Reset Root Password" under the Settings tab. A new password is generated and emailed to the address on file within a few minutes.

How do I contact support?

Email help@nimbuscloud.dev or use the live chat widget in the dashboard. Include your server ID so the team can look up your account faster.
